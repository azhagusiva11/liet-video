LIET.05 / 07
04 · The safety net

It catches what's easy to miss.

Quietly, in the background — as suggestions you can act on or ignore. Never decisions.

⚠
Drug-interaction flags
If two medicines clash — like an NSAID with a blood-pressure drug — LIET flags it for your review against an Indian drug-brand database.
◷
Pertinent negatives & checks
What the patient denied is captured too. Dose limits and contradictions are surfaced so nothing slips through.
✓
You stay in control
Every flag is a suggestion in a clearly-labelled panel — separate from your note. You decide what matters.
